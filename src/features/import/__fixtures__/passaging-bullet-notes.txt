Passaging HEK293T cells
Notes from Monday, adapted from our lab wiki
- Warm DMEM + 10% FBS and trypsin to 37 °C in the water bath
- Aspirate medium from the flask, wash once with 5 mL PBS
- Add 1 mL 0.25% trypsin-EDTA, rock the flask, incubate 3 min at 37 °C
- Check under the microscope, cells should be detached
- Neutralize with 4 mL warm medium and pipette up and down
- Spin at 300 g for 5 min, aspirate the supernatant
- Resuspend the pellet in 10 mL fresh medium and count cells
- Seed 1.5 x 10^6 cells into a new T75 flask with 15 mL medium
- Put back in the 37 °C incubator, 5% CO2
Note: do not let cells sit in trypsin longer than 5 min or they clump.
