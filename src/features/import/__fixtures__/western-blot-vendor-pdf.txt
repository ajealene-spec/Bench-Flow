Protein Detection by Western Blot
Thermo Fisher Scientific | Protocol guide
For Research Use Only. Not for use in diagnostic procedures.
Introduction
This guide describes how to transfer separated proteins to a membrane and detect a target with antibodies. Read the full guide before you start.
Materials and equipment
- Running buffer (1X Tris-glycine SDS)
- Transfer buffer, 20% methanol
- PVDF membrane, 0.45 µm
- Blocking solution: 5% non-fat milk in TBST
- Primary antibody, anti-GAPDH, 1:5000
- HRP-conjugated secondary antibody
- Chemiluminescent substrate (ECL)
- Electrophoresis tank and power supply
- Wet transfer apparatus
- Chemiluminescence imager
Procedure
1. Separate the protein lysate on an SDS-PAGE gel at 120 V for 90 min.
2. Assemble the transfer sandwich in the cassette, with sponges and filter paper soaked in transfer buffer.
3. Transfer at 100 V for 1 h in an ice-cold wet transfer apparatus.
4. Block the membrane in 5% milk in TBST for 1 h at room temperature.
5. Incubate with primary antibody (1:5000) in blocking solution overnight at 4 °C.
6. Wash the membrane three times for 5 min each in TBST.
7. Incubate with HRP secondary antibody (1:10000) for 1 h at room temperature.
8. Wash three times for 5 min in TBST.
9. Apply ECL substrate and image on a chemiluminescence imager.
Caution: Methanol in transfer buffer is flammable and toxic. Wear gloves and work in a fume hood.
