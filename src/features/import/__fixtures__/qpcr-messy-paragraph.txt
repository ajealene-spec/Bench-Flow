qPCR to quantify target gene expression from cDNA. so first we extract RNA with TRIzol, then like make cDNA using the RT kit (1 µg RNA, 20 µL reaction) and then the qPCR. Thaw the SYBR Green master mix on ice and keep it dark. Dilute cDNA 1:10 in nuclease-free water. Mix 5 µL SYBR Green, 0.5 µL each primer (10 µM), 2 µL diluted cDNA and 2 µL water per well in a 96-well plate. Seal it with optical film and spin down at 1000 rpm for 1 min. Run on the thermocycler: 95 °C for 10 min, then 40 cycles of 95 °C for 15 s and 60 °C for 1 min. Melt curve from 65 to 95 °C. Include NTC and no-RT controls. Analyze with the 2^-ΔΔCt method, normalized to GAPDH. Its important to keep everything cold. Caution: TRIzol contains phenol, wear gloves and work in the hood.
