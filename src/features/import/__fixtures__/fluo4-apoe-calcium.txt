Calcium imaging of APOE astrocytes with Fluo-4
Purpose: to measure intracellular calcium responses of primary astrocytes carrying APOE3 or APOE4 to glutamate and alpha-synuclein.
Materials
- Matrigel
- Laminin, 15 µL
- Fluo-4 AM, 5 µM
- Tris-HCl buffer
- Glutamate, 5 µM
- Alpha-synuclein (α-syn), 100 nM and 500 nM
- FIJI (ImageJ) with the ROI manager
- Fluorescence microscope with a 30 fps camera
- Glass-bottom dishes
Procedure
1. Coat glass-bottom dishes with Matrigel and 15 µL laminin.
2. Culture astrocytes until they reach 80% confluency.
3. Load cells with 5 µM Fluo-4 AM for 1 h at 37 °C, then 30 min at room temperature.
4. Wash twice with Tris-HCl buffer.
5. Image at 30 fps. Record a 5 s baseline, then add the treatment, then image for 1 min.
6. Treatments: Tris-HCl buffer (vehicle), 5 µM glutamate, α-syn 100 nM, α-syn 500 nM.
7. Analyze in FIJI: draw ROIs around each cell, compute ΔF/F0 and the area under the curve (AUC).
8. Compare treatments with t-tests.
