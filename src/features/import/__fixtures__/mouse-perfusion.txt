Transcardial perfusion and brain collection in mice
Purpose: prepare fixed brain tissue from adult C57BL/6 mice for histology.
Materials
- Isoflurane
- Ketamine/xylazine cocktail
- Ice-cold PBS
- 4% paraformaldehyde in PBS
- Peristaltic pump
- Surgical scissors and forceps
- 25G butterfly needles
- Sucrose solution, 30%
Procedure
1. Anesthetize the mouse with isoflurane until the toe pinch reflex is absent.
2. Open the thoracic cavity and expose the heart.
3. Insert the needle into the left ventricle and clamp the right atrium.
4. Perfuse with ice-cold PBS for 5 min at 8 mL/min, then with 4% PFA for 10 min.
5. Dissect the brain and post-fix in 4% PFA overnight at 4 °C.
6. Transfer the brain to 30% sucrose for 48 h at 4 °C until it sinks.
Caution: PFA is toxic. Dispose of sharps in the sharps container.
