Protein Detection by Western Blot
Western blotting separates proteins by size, moves them onto a membrane and detects one target with a specific antibody. This guide covers a standard workflow for cultured mammalian cells. Read the safety data sheets for every reagent before you begin.
Background on the chemistry is summarised in the first chapter of the handbook.

Procedure
Sample preparation
1. Wash the cell monolayer twice with ice-cold PBS.
2. Add 200 µL ice-cold RIPA lysis buffer per well of a 6-well plate, and scrape the cells.
Caution: RIPA contains SDS and deoxycholate. Wear gloves and eye protection.
3. Incubate the lysate on ice for 20 min, mixing gently.
4. Centrifuge at 14,000 × g for 15 min at 4 °C and transfer the supernatant to a fresh tube.
5. Measure protein concentration with a BCA assay. Record the concentration in µg/µL.
Keep lysates on ice
SDS-PAGE
6. Mix 20 µg of protein with 4x Laemmli buffer and heat at 95 °C for 5 min.
7. Load samples onto a 4–12% Bis-Tris gel and run at 150 V for 1 h in MES running buffer.
Protein transfer
8. Assemble the transfer sand-
wich in the cassette and transfer to PVDF membrane at 30 V for 90 min in cold transfer buffer.
9. Stain the membrane with Ponceau S for 5 min to check transfer, then rinse with TBST.

Blocking
10. Block the membrane in 5% non-fat milk in TBST for 1 h at room temperature with gentle agitation.
Antibody incubation
11. Dilute the primary antibody 1:1000 in 5% BSA in TBST. Incubate the membrane overnight at 4 °C on a rocker.
12. Wash the membrane 3 x 5 min in TBST.
13. Dilute the HRP-conjugated secondary antibody 1:5000 in blocking buffer and incubate for 1 h at room temperature.
14. Wash the membrane 3 x 5 min in TBST, then once for 5 min in TBS.
Detection
15. Incubate the membrane with ECL substrate for 1 min, then image with a chemiluminescence imager.
16. Record the exposure time and band intensity values in the notebook.
Troubleshooting
Weak signal: increase the primary antibody concentration or the incubation time.
High background: increase the number of washes or the blocking time.
No bands: confirm transfer with Ponceau S and check the antibody datasheet.
